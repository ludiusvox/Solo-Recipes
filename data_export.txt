1. Old Fashioned Apple Pie
   Ingredients: 3/4 cup Sugar, 2 tbsp Flour, 1/2 tsp Cinnamon, 1/4 tsp Nutmeg, 1/2 tsp Lemon Zest, 7 cups thinly sliced Apples, 2 tsp Lemon Juice, 1 tbsp Butter, 1 recipe Pastry for 9-inch double crust, 4 tbsp Milk
   Directions:
   - Preheat oven to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C).
   - Mix together the sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg and lemon peel.
   - Line one crust in a 9-inch deep-dish pie pan. Layer 1/3 of apples into pie crust. Sprinkle with sugar mixture and repeat until done. Sprinkle with lemon juice and dot with butter.
   - Place second pie crust on top of filling and flute the edges. Cut vents in top crust and brush with milk.
   - Bake at 425 degrees F (220 degrees C) for 40 to 50 minutes.

2. The Best Banana Bread
   Ingredients: 1 stick Butter, 2 cups Flour, 1/2 tsp Salt, 1 1/2 tsp Baking Powder, 1 cup Sugar, 3 very ripe Bananas, 2 Eggs, 1 tsp Vanilla Extract
   Directions:
   - Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
   - Whisk together the flour, salt, baking powder, and sugar.
   - Mix together the melted butter and mashed bananas. Beat in the eggs and vanilla. Stir into the dry ingredients.
   - Pour the batter into the prepared pan. Bake for 50 to 60 minutes.

3. Classic Savory Meatloaf
   Ingredients: 2 lbs lean ground Beef, 1 (10.75 oz) can Condensed Tomato Soup, 1 Onion, finely diced, 1 cup Rolled Oats, 2 Eggs, beaten, 1/2 cup Ketchup, 3 tbsp Mustard
   Directions:
   - In a large bowl, combine meat, soup, onion, oats, and eggs. Mix well.
   - Shape into a loaf, and pat into a loaf pan.
   - Bake in a preheated 350 degrees F (175 degrees C) oven for 1 hour.

4. Lemon Garlic Grilled Salmon
   Ingredients: 4 Salmon Fillets, 2 tbsp Lemon Juice, 2 tbsp Butter, melted, 2 cloves Garlic, minced, 1 tsp dried Dill Weed
   Directions:
   - Season salmon fillets with salt and pepper.
   - Whisk together lemon juice, butter, garlic, and dill.
   - Grill salmon for 5 to 7 minutes per side, brushing with butter mixture.

5. Creamy Chicken Alfredo
   Ingredients: 1 lb Fettuccine, 1 lb Chicken Breast, 1/2 cup Butter, 1 pint Heavy Cream, 1/2 cup Parmesan Cheese, 2 cloves Garlic, minced
   Directions:
   - Cook pasta according to package directions.
   - Sauté chicken in butter until cooked through.
   - Simmer cream and garlic in a pan. Stir in cheese until melted. Toss with pasta and chicken.

6. Hearty Beef Stew
   Ingredients: 2 lbs Beef Chuck, cubed, 4 large Potatoes, cubed, 4 Carrots, sliced, 1 Onion, chopped, 4 cups Beef Broth, 1 tsp dried Thyme
   Directions:
   - Brown beef in a large pot.
   - Add onion, potatoes, carrots, broth, and thyme.
   - Simmer on low for 1.5 to 2 hours until meat is tender.

7. Crispy Caesar Salad
   Ingredients: head Romaine Lettuce, 1 cup Croutons, 1/4 cup Parmesan Cheese, 1/2 cup Caesar Dressing, 1 tsp Black Pepper
   Directions:
   - Wash and tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces.
   - Toss lettuce with dressing until well coated.
   - Top with croutons, cheese, and freshly cracked black pepper.

8. The Ultimate Chocolate Chip Cookie
   Ingredients: 1 cup Butter, 1 cup White Sugar, 1 cup Brown Sugar, 2 Eggs, 2 tsp Vanilla Extract, 3 cups Flour, 1 tsp Baking Soda, 2 cups Chocolate Chips
   Directions:
   - Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
   - Cream together butter and sugars. Beat in eggs and vanilla.
   - Mix in dry ingredients and stir in chocolate chips.
   - Bake for 10 minutes or until edges are golden.

9. Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes
   Ingredients: 1 1/2 cups Flour, 1 tbsp Sugar, 1 tsp Salt, 1 tsp Baking Powder, 1/2 tsp Baking Soda, 1 1/4 cups Buttermilk, 1 Egg, 3 tbsp Butter, melted
   Directions:
   - Whisk together dry ingredients.
   - Whisk together buttermilk, egg, and butter. Combine with dry ingredients.
   - Cook on a lightly oiled griddle until bubbles form and pop.

10. Easy Homemade Guacamole
    Ingredients: 3 Avocados, mashed, 1 Lime, juiced, 1 tsp Salt, 1/2 cup Onion, diced, 3 tbsp Cilantro, chopped, 2 Roma Tomatoes, diced
    Directions:
    - In a medium bowl, mash together the avocados, lime juice, and salt.
    - Mix in onion, cilantro, and tomatoes.
    - Refrigerate for 1 hour for best flavor, or serve immediately.
