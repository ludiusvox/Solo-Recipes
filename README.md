# Solo-Recipes: The Flavor Stitcher

## The Rationale
The primary inspiration for this app comes from a simple, personal reality: **I have no idea how to season food.**

While there are thousands of apps that tell you how to cook a steak or follow a complex recipe, there aren't many that focus specifically on the *art of flavoring*. Solo-Recipes is designed to be a **suggestive companion** for your kitchen. It's not just about the meat or the cooking time; it's about figuring out which herbs, spices, and condiments will actually make your food taste better.

I found cookbooks from gutenburg and compiled 800 recipes that work

Whether you're looking at a piece of chicken, a bowl of leafy greens, or a tray of root vegetables, this app "stitches" together the right flavor profiles to help you build confidence in your seasoning choices.

## Key Features
- **Visual Kitchen Inventory**: Keep track of your spices and herbs. Toggle items "In Stock" or "Out of Stock" to see how they match with your favorite recipes.
- **Visual Shopping List**: Need to buy a missing spice? Add it to your shopping list, snap a photo of the brand you prefer using your phone's camera, and keep that visual catalog when it moves into your pantry.
- **Custom Recipe Bank**: Add your own recipes with full photo support, instructions, and ingredient lists.

## Why "Solo-Recipes"?
This is your solo journey into mastering flavor. It's about removing the guesswork from the spice rack and helping you create meals that are consistently well-seasoned.

## Tech Stack
- **Language**: Kotlin
- **UI**: Jetpack Compose (Material 3)
- **Image Loading**: Coil
- **Design System**: Google Stitch
- **Architecture**: Modern Android MVVM principles with Local JSON data sourcing.
